## 01 / What this notice covers

This Privacy Notice explains how ILHAM IZANY RESOURCES, operating the HopeXito marketplace, collects, uses, stores, and discloses personal data when you visit HopeXito, create an account, publish work, or use the checkout flow.

This notice is written for the current HopeXito product and is intended to be read with Malaysia's [Personal Data Protection Act 2010](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/pdp-act-2010-en/), as amended from time to time.

## 02 / The information we receive

Depending on how you use HopeXito, we may collect:

- **Account data:** your name, email address, password credentials, verification status, and profile photo.
- **Profile and creator data:** biography, public profile details, product titles, tags, artwork, product images, prices, and publishing settings.
- **Order data:** recipient name, email, phone number, delivery address, postcode, state, selected size and colour, order history, and fulfilment status.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser and device information, session data, pages visited, and basic diagnostic logs.
- **Communication data:** messages or requests you send to support and records needed to respond to them.

Never send full payment card numbers through a support message or product upload. The current checkout is a payment simulation and does not process or store card details.

## 03 / Why we use it

We use personal data to:

1. create and secure accounts, authenticate logins, and verify email addresses;
2. operate profiles, product studios, product pages, carts, checkout, orders, and creator workspaces;
3. display public creator information and published work as you choose;
4. communicate about account activity, orders, security, support, and important service changes;
5. prevent abuse, investigate suspicious activity, enforce our Terms of Service, and keep the marketplace reliable;
6. understand how the site is used, fix problems, and improve the experience; and
7. comply with legal, accounting, tax, dispute-resolution, and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use private artwork or delivery details as public marketing content without a valid reason and appropriate permission.

## 04 / What is public

If you publish a creator profile or product, the information needed to present that work may be visible to other visitors. This can include your display name, profile photo, biography, product title, tags, product images, product type, price, and published status.

Your email address, password, phone number, delivery address, and order details are not displayed as part of a public product page.

## 05 / Service providers and disclosures

We may share limited personal data with providers that help us run HopeXito, such as hosting and storage providers, authentication and email providers, analytics providers, delivery partners, and payment or order-processing providers when those services are enabled.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect users, protect HopeXito, investigate fraud or abuse, or handle a dispute. We require service providers to use the information only for the services they provide to us and to protect it appropriately.

Some providers may process information outside Malaysia. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate contractual, technical, and organisational safeguards.

## 06 / Cookies and analytics

HopeXito uses cookies and similar technologies to keep sessions working, remember essential preferences, protect forms, and understand site usage. Blocking essential cookies may affect sign-in, cart, checkout, or other functionality.

Where analytics are enabled, they may receive technical and usage information about visits to help us measure and improve the service. We do not use analytics to read private artwork files or payment credentials.

## 07 / Retention and security

We keep personal data only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including account operation, order history, legal obligations, dispute handling, and security. When it is no longer needed, we will delete it, anonymise it, or securely restrict access where appropriate.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards. No online service can promise absolute security, so please use a unique password and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

## 08 / Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law and reasonable verification, you may ask us to:

- provide access to personal data we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
- withdraw consent where processing relies on consent;
- stop using your information for direct marketing; and
- delete or restrict information where retention is no longer required or permitted.

Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, accounting, or dispute purposes. We may also need to keep a limited record of a request so we can respect it.

Send requests or privacy questions to [support@hopexito.com](mailto:support@hopexito.com). Please use the email connected to your account where possible and describe the request clearly. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity.

## 09 / Children

HopeXito is not intended for children who cannot lawfully agree to these terms. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact support so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

## 10 / Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the service, our providers, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use personal data, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to your attention.

## 11 / Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or complaints, contact [support@hopexito.com](mailto:support@hopexito.com). You may also contact the Personal Data Protection Commissioner in Malaysia if you believe your concern has not been resolved.
